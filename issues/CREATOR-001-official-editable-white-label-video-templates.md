# CREATOR-001 — Templates: Editable white-label GHL videos

**Product:** Creator Studio
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** New
**Impact:** High
**Date reported:** 2026-11-19
**Source:** [November 19 Product Town Hall feedback and live follow-up](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/townhall-2026-11-19-creator-tracking-media-evidence.md)

## Observed

HighLevel publishes generic feature and product videos that many agencies reuse unchanged. Ivo **reported requesting verbally during the November 19 Town Hall** a supply of official **editable white-label videos for HighLevel products and features**, distributed through template links and customized inside Creator Studio. In chat at 18:27:56 he clarified that template links should be **agency-scoped**, not visible to every sub-account. At 18:28–18:29 he confirmed that starting from blank/template is possible today but harder to discover than starting with AI.

Other participants reported that targeted prompt edits sometimes change unrelated design elements. They did not report or demonstrate that every Creator Studio project has this defect. A HighLevel representative confirmed custom sizes for blank projects exist. When a separate user asked to import templates from other sub-accounts, Shivangi answered “Noted,” **not an ETA or a commitment to official white-label video templates**.

Existing HighLevel **email** share links, generic Template Library and **AI Studio** cloning should be reused as design precedents, but none proves this capability in **Creator Studio** today.

## Why it matters

Agencies want unique, branded, localized product education and sales demonstrations rather than identical official GHL videos or recreating every tutorial from scratch. Requiring an exported MP4 or a fresh generative session defeats editable template distribution, raises cost and creates unnecessary design drift.

## Expected

1. HighLevel provides **official, legally white-labelable, up-to-date product/feature demo video projects**, organized by product/topic and supported version. Agencies can import them via **share link** into Creator Studio as editable source projects, not just flat MP4 exports.
2. Templates expose reusable video scenes/timeline/layers and editable agency logo, colors, fonts, screenshots/product names, CTA, links, narration/voiceover, captions and language. Users can replace content without replacing unrelated scenes.
3. **Agency-first permissions**: share links and project copies are available to eligible agency admins/users with optional explicit sharing to selected sub-accounts. No automatic publishing to all client accounts.
4. Preserve independent agency-branded forks, original source/version/attribution and optional upstream update prompts; do not silently overwrite published or customized projects.
5. Offer a clear **Use official template / Start blank / Start with AI** entry point, with preview and role/plan/usage information, so nontechnical agency users find it.
6. Publish straightforward commercial usage and licensing terms: permission to edit, rebrand, localize, republish and sell agency-specific onboarding/sales videos, and restrictions around HighLevel trademarks/product imagery.

**Minimum acceptance demo:** HighLevel shares three representative editable product videos (e.g. CRM, Workflows, AI Studio). An agency imports one via a link, changes brand, CTA, language and a scene, previews, exports a working final in common aspect ratios and shares only with selected authorized users, all without an external video editor or unrelated AI changes.

## HighLevel response

Shivangi Awasthi acknowledged another attendee's question about **importing templates from other sub-accounts** with “Noted” in the November 19 chat. This **does not** confirm official editable white-label product-video templates. A 30-minute discussion with Shivangi is booked for November 20, 2026; no roadmap commitment or outcome has been established.
