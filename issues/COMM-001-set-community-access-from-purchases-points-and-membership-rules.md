# COMM-001 — Access: Grant membership from purchases

**Product:** Communities
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** New
**Impact:** High
**Date reported:** 2026-05-22
**Source:** [Zoom feedback index](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/zoom-feedback-index-2026.md) — 2026-05-22 Product Town Hall

## Observed
Ivo asked for access conditions without duplicating separate workflows/funnels for each rule.

## Why it matters
Access/entitlement rules do not scale when every variation needs another funnel or workflow.

## Expected
Allow Community access to be granted or revoked from purchases, products/offers, points, tags, or membership rules without duplicating funnels/workflows for each case.

## HighLevel response
No direct response or verified implementation recorded.

## Official changelog verification — 2026-11-13

**Result:** Partial implementation — request remains open.

**Already available:** Community subscription plans can grant bundled access to selected channels, paid events and paid/private courses; members can switch plans.

**Scope decision:** Generic purchases/products/offers, tags, points and grant/revoke precedence across external purchase routes are not established.

**Official sources:** [Multiple Pricing Plans in Communities](https://ideas.gohighlevel.com/changelog/multiple-pricing-plans-in-communities).

**Checked:** 2026-11-13. A feature announced only in Labs/private beta is not automatically generally available.
