# MR-222 — Microphone: Explain browser permissions

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** UX
**Status:** Acknowledged
**Date reported:** 2026-11-13
**Source:** [Team kollab roadmap discussion, 2026-11-13](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/meeting-room-roadmap-community-discussion-2026-11-13.md)
**Impact:** Medium

## Observed
A community participant reported a Windows Chrome attendee entering without first activating the microphone and missing the browser's permission prompt, then not understanding how to activate the mic.

## Why it matters
First-time attendees may appear muted or think audio is broken, while browsers present different permission interfaces.

## Expected
Detect missing/denied microphone permissions, show a clear in-room guided explanation with browser-sensitive instructions and a retry button, and distinguish browser permission from in-room mute state.

## HighLevel response
Praveen said he would check/reproduce and was discussing a possible solution with the internal team. **No committed feature or date**.
