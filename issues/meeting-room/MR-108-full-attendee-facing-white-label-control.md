# MR-108 — Full attendee-facing white-label control

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Acknowledged
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)
**Impact:** High


## Observed
Custom domains exist, but attendee-facing room surfaces still need broader branding control.

## Why it matters
Agencies should be able to make the meeting experience feel native to each brand.

## Expected
Agency/location/room overrides for logo, favicon, page title, colors, waiting-room content and other attendee-facing UI.

## HighLevel response
HighLevel noted the feedback and will account for broader white-label customization across attendee-facing Meeting Room experiences.

## Live webinar test follow-up — 2026-11-13

**Source:** [Sanitized test notes](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/meeting-room-webinar-test-2026-11-13.md)

The 2026-11-13 room test found no configurable join-page or waiting-room copy, instructions or CTA buttons. Request self-service, per-room/funnel controls for these screens. Post-meeting redirect actions are tracked separately as MR-215, rather than folding funnel conversion behavior into branding. Increase impact to High for webinar use.
