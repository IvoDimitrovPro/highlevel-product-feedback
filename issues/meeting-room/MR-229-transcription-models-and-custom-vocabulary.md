# MR-229 — Transcription: Models and custom vocabulary

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** New
**Impact:** High
**Date reported:** 2026-11-21
**Source:** [Meeting Room Recording Sharing release and discussion](https://www.gokollab.com/meeting-rooms/home/posts/6b00729921bd52751e5d120a?commentId=6b0072e01ed0c5179d757433); [evidence summary](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/meeting-room-recording-sharing-release-2026-11-20.md)

## Observed

Although the current Deepgram Nova-3 transcript supports Bulgarian, Ivo said Parakeet performed better for his Bulgarian speech in his own local tests. Company, brand and recurring domain names are also often mistranscribed.

## Why it matters

Client-facing recording sharing should be controllable, accurate and usable within a white-label agency product, without exposing media or delivering misleading summary content.

## Expected

Offer a quality-oriented language/model profile (optionally selectable or auto chosen), document chosen model and version, enable custom vocabulary/word hints/pronunciation including Cyrillic brands, and permit reprocessing a past recording while retaining the source. Parakeet is a suggested option, not an existing HighLevel integration.

## HighLevel response

Deepgram Nova-3 and language selection were previously confirmed. No comment reply confirms model switching or custom vocabulary.

## Verification

Reviewed the authenticated GoKollab product announcement and comments on 2026-11-21. Specific new functionality has not been demonstrated as shipped. Do not confuse a planned improvement or an acknowledgement with delivery.
