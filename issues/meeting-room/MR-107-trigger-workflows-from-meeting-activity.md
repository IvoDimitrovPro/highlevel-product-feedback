# MR-107 — Trigger Workflows from meeting activity

**Product:** Workflows
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Planned
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)
**Impact:** High

## Observed
Attendance and meeting outcomes should support automated follow-up.

## Why it matters
Workflow automation needs meeting events to react to attendance, no-shows, and post-session assets.

## Expected
Expose native Workflow triggers/actions for meeting registration, join/no-show, attendance duration, recording/transcript readiness, and other session outcomes.

## HighLevel response
Meeting/session events are already planned for Workflows to trigger follow-ups and automations.

## Live webinar test follow-up — 2026-11-13

**Source:** [Sanitized test notes](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/meeting-room-webinar-test-2026-11-13.md)

Prioritize webinar triggers based on verified Meeting Room participation (joined, left, duration, no-show and recording/transcript ready), linked to the correct contact and funnel session. HighLevel already provides funnel registration/reminder workflows; the missing requested scope is **room-level attendance-driven** automation rather than generic registration triggers.
