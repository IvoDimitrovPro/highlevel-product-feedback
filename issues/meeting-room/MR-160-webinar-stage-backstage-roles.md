# MR-160 — Webinars: Presenter and audience roles

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Acknowledged
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)
**Impact:** High


## Observed
A webinar needs production/stage behavior different from a normal gallery meeting.

## Why it matters
Speakers need a private preparation area and the audience needs controlled participation.

## Expected
Backstage/practice area; host/co-host/producer/panelist/audience roles; bring speakers on/off stage; audience mic/camera off by default.

## HighLevel response
HighLevel agrees webinars need dedicated roles and stage/audience behavior distinct from standard meetings.

**New 2026-11-13 reply:** A dedicated webinar UI/experience, with improved host controls and a smoother attendee experience, is broadly planned. The specific stage/backstage and role requirements of MR-160 have no explicit commitment. [Source](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-webinar-response-2026-11-13.md).

## Live webinar test follow-up — 2026-11-13

**Source:** [Sanitized test notes](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/meeting-room-webinar-test-2026-11-13.md)

The 2026-11-13 host panel shows general meeting permissions (self-unmute, camera, share screen, reactions, raise hand), but a webinar needs a reusable attendee view-only preset, a presenter/host role, and audience-focused layout. See MR-211 for content-only fullscreen and MR-213 for annotation permission. This is a use-case-specific follow-up, not evidence of a new HighLevel commitment.
