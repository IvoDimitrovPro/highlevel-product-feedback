# MR-216 — Remember attendee name and device preferences on rejoin

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Bug
**Status:** Acknowledged
**Date reported:** 2026-11-13
**Source:** [Sanitized live-webinar test, 2026-11-13](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/meeting-room-webinar-test-2026-11-13.md); [HighLevel reply](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-webinar-response-2026-11-13.md)
**Impact:** Medium

## Observed
After leaving and rejoining the tested room, the attendee had to re-enter their display name and audio/video preferences.

## Why it matters
Reconnections add avoidable friction and increase the chance of joining with the wrong devices or mute state.

## Expected
Remember the name, selected devices, and camera/microphone preferences across rejoin where browser permissions allow, with privacy-safe session storage and clear user controls.

## HighLevel response
Praveen Gupta (Team kollab), 2026-11-13: All feedback points noted. A dedicated webinar UI and experience, with improved host controls and attendee UX, is planned broadly. This specific request is not individually committed or dated. [Source](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-webinar-response-2026-11-13.md).
