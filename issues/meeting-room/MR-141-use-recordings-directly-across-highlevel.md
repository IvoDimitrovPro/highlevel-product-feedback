# MR-141 — Recordings: Reuse across HighLevel

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Acknowledged
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)
**Impact:** High


## Observed
A recording should not need download/re-upload to be reused.

## Why it matters
The same recording may belong to CRM, Community, Course, Event, Media Storage or Webinar.

## Expected
Attach to Contact/Appointment/Opportunity; publish to Community; add to Course/Event; use as Webinar Replay; save/copy to Media Storage.

## HighLevel response
HighLevel intends recordings to be reusable across its products, including sharing or attaching them to relevant entities.

## November 2026 roadmap cross-reference

Praveen announced generic Recording Sharing planned for the week of November 16–22, 2026. This may help this reusable-asset workflow, but **does not commit** to embedding/attaching a recording across CRM, Courses, Events, Webinars and Media Storage. Keep Acknowledged. [Source](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/meeting-room-roadmap-community-discussion-2026-11-13.md).

## Official changelog verification — 2026-11-13

**Result:** Partial implementation — request remains open.

**Already available:** Recording/history assets exist in Meeting Rooms.

**Scope decision:** Direct reuse across Contacts, Community, Course, Webinar, and Media Storage without download/re-upload is not verified.

**Official sources:** [Introducing Meeting Room](https://ideas.gohighlevel.com/changelog/introducing-meeting-room).

**Checked:** 2026-11-13. A feature announced only in Labs/private beta is not automatically generally available.

## Town Hall follow-up — November 16, 2026

Praveen Gupta replied to another participant on November 16: **Media Library integration is planned**, and **recording sharing including chat/transcription was targeted for that same week**. This is a narrower roadmap statement than the original cross-product recording reuse requirement; no confirmed shipped Media Library link, stable asset identity, permissions or automatic update propagation was demonstrated. [Evidence](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/townhall-media-tracking-widget-evidence-2026-11-19.md).
