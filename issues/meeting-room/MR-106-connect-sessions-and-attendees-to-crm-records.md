# MR-106 — CRM: Link attendees and sessions

**Product:** CRM
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Planned
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)
**Impact:** High

## Observed
Relevant session data should be usable from related contact/opportunity history.

## Why it matters
CRM-native meeting data is required for useful follow-up and is a key advantage over standalone meeting tools.

## Expected
Store relevant attendance, recording, transcript, AI notes, and engagement data on the related Contact, Appointment, and Opportunity records.

## HighLevel response
Attendance, recordings, transcripts, AI notes, and engagement data are already planned for integration with relevant CRM entities.

## Live webinar test follow-up — 2026-11-13

**Source:** [Sanitized test notes](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/meeting-room-webinar-test-2026-11-13.md)

The live webinar test highlights a join-to-contact identity gap: the direct Meeting Room join page only asks for a display name, whereas webinar funnels already support [CRM-linked registration forms](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000004125). Need a reliable association from verified registered contact to room participant and attendance duration, without requiring a second manual form. The join-fields/prefill UI belongs to MR-214.
