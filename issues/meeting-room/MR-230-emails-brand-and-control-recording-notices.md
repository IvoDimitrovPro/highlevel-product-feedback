# MR-230 — Emails: Brand and control recording notices

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Acknowledged
**Impact:** Medium
**Date reported:** 2026-11-21
**Source:** [Meeting Room Recording Sharing release and discussion](https://www.gokollab.com/meeting-rooms/home/posts/6b00729921bd52751e5d120a?commentId=6b0072e01ed0c5179d757433); [evidence summary](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/meeting-room-recording-sharing-release-2026-11-20.md)

## Observed

In the Recording Sharing thread, Marcel Kleusener asked to customize the recipient email branding/content for white-label SaaS and to disable repetitive recording-ready email notifications to hosts.

## Why it matters

Client-facing recording sharing should be controllable, accurate and usable within a white-label agency product, without exposing media or delivering misleading summary content.

## Expected

Provide editable share email subject, brand/theme, sender (within deliverability and permission constraints), and default templates per agency/subaccount. Let hosts or admins opt out of nonessential recording-ready emails separately from recipient and security notices.

## HighLevel response

Praveen confirmed contacts selected from the picker receive email, and email customization is NOT yet available; the team will plan it soon, without a date. Host notification opt-out has not been confirmed.

## Verification

Reviewed the authenticated GoKollab product announcement and comments on 2026-11-21. Specific new functionality has not been demonstrated as shipped. Do not confuse a planned improvement or an acknowledgement with delivery.
