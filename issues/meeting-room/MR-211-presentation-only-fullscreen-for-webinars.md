# MR-211 — Presentation-only fullscreen without participant tiles

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** UX
**Status:** New
**Date reported:** 2026-11-13
**Source:** [Sanitized live-webinar test, 2026-11-13](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/meeting-room-webinar-test-2026-11-13.md)
**Impact:** High

## Observed
Screen sharing in the tested room kept participant tiles/avatars visible and reduced the area available for the presentation, even in expanded layout.

## Why it matters
Attendees should focus on content, not mostly empty participant video tiles.

## Expected
Provide a content-only fullscreen view for viewers, with unobtrusive controls and an easy way back to the standard meeting layout. Differentiate this from browser fullscreen itself.

## HighLevel response
No response to this specific 2026-11-13 observation recorded.

## Verification
Reported from one account's live test on 2026-11-13; not verified as a platform-wide limitation. Screenshots remain private because they contain attendee identity and meeting access information.
