# MR-225 — Defaults: Configure subaccount meetings

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Planned
**Date reported:** 2026-11-13
**Source:** [Team kollab roadmap discussion, 2026-11-13](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/meeting-room-roadmap-community-discussion-2026-11-13.md)
**Impact:** High

## Observed
Account administrators need shared defaults across all future sessions, rather than configuring each meeting repeatedly; a global defaults page is not equivalent to per-room presets.

## Why it matters
Consistent meeting security, co-hosts and capture configuration reduce mistakes across teams.

## Expected
Add a sub-account-wide Default Meeting Settings page, including co-hosts, automatic recording, open meeting access and automatic transcription; document precedence between global defaults, room presets and per-meeting overrides.

Praveen on 2026-11-13: **Default Meeting Settings** planned for **November 2026**, with the four example controls explicitly named. No exact release date.
