# MR-224 — Rooms: Create multiple personal rooms

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Planned
**Date reported:** 2026-11-13
**Source:** [Team kollab roadmap discussion, 2026-11-13](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/meeting-room-roadmap-community-discussion-2026-11-13.md)
**Impact:** High

## Observed
Current Meeting Room setup allows one personal room plus team rooms; users cannot freely create and manage multiple personal rooms according to Praveen's description.

## Why it matters
Different clients, webinars and recurring events benefit from distinct permanent links, policies, branding and schedules.

## Expected
Allow each authorized user to create, name, configure, manage and retire multiple meeting rooms with independent links and permissions.

## HighLevel response
Praveen on 2026-11-13: **Multiple Meeting Rooms** is planned for **November 2026**. No exact date or detailed limits provided.
