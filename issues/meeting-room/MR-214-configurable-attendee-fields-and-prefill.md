# MR-214 — Configurable direct-join fields and prefilled attendee identity

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** New
**Date reported:** 2026-11-13
**Source:** [Sanitized live-webinar test, 2026-11-13](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/meeting-room-webinar-test-2026-11-13.md)
**Impact:** High

## Observed
The tested direct room join flow requested a display name but did not offer configurable email/phone fields. A webinar registrant was not automatically recognized/prefilled in this flow.

## Why it matters
Direct-link hosts cannot capture the contact information they need; registered users may need to enter details twice.

## Expected
Offer configurable join fields mapped to CRM, and securely prefill or skip them for authenticated/identified webinar registrants. Distinguish public link visitors from verified contacts and avoid exposing personal data in public URLs.

## HighLevel response
No response to this specific 2026-11-13 observation recorded.

## Verification
Reported from one account's live test on 2026-11-13; not verified as a platform-wide limitation. Screenshots remain private because they contain attendee identity and meeting access information.
