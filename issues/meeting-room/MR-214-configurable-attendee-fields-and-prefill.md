# MR-214 — Configurable direct-join fields and prefilled attendee identity

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Acknowledged
**Date reported:** 2026-11-13
**Source:** [Sanitized live-webinar test, 2026-11-13](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/meeting-room-webinar-test-2026-11-13.md); [HighLevel reply](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-webinar-response-2026-11-13.md)
**Impact:** High

## Observed
The tested direct room join flow requested a display name but did not offer configurable email/phone fields. Automatic prefilling for a previously registered webinar attendee was requested but not independently tested.

## Why it matters
Direct-link hosts cannot capture the contact information they need; registered users may need to enter details twice.

## Expected
Offer configurable join fields mapped to CRM, and securely prefill or skip them for authenticated/identified webinar registrants. Distinguish public link visitors from verified contacts and avoid exposing personal data in public URLs.

## HighLevel response
Praveen Gupta (Team kollab), 2026-11-13: All feedback points noted. A dedicated webinar UI and experience, with improved host controls and attendee UX, is planned broadly. This specific request is not individually committed or dated. [Source](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-webinar-response-2026-11-13.md).
