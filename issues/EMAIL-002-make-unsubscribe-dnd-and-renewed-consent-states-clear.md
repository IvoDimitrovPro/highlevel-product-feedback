# EMAIL-002 — Consent: Clear unsubscribe and DND states

**Product:** Email / Contacts
**Type:** UX
**Status:** New
**Impact:** High
**Date reported:** 2026-05-06
**Source:** [Zoom feedback index](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/zoom-feedback-index-2026.md) — 2026-05-06 Product Town Hall

## Observed
Ivo reported confusion between DND and unsubscribe and unclear state when a contact opts in again.

## Why it matters
Ambiguous consent states can cause incorrect suppression/sending and create avoidable compliance risk.

## Expected
Show unsubscribe, DND, and consent as distinct understandable states; when a contact re-consents, update the state predictably and preserve the history.

## HighLevel response
No fix established in the messages reviewed.

## Official changelog verification — 2026-11-13

**Result:** Partial implementation — request remains open.

**Already available:** Community members can manage individual Community notification email subscriptions without unsubscribing from other areas.

**Scope decision:** Contact-level DND, marketing unsubscribe, re-consent and auditable state transitions remain separate requirements.

**Official sources:** [Granular unsubscribe controls for Community emails](https://ideas.gohighlevel.com/changelog/granular-unsubscribe-controls-for-community-emails).

**Checked:** 2026-11-13. A feature announced only in Labs/private beta is not automatically generally available.
