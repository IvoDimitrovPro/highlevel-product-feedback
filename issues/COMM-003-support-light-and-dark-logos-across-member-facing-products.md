# COMM-003 — Branding: Light and dark logos

**Product:** Communities / Courses
**Type:** Branding
**Status:** New
**Impact:** Medium
**Date reported:** 2026-05-22
**Source:** [Zoom feedback index](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/zoom-feedback-index-2026.md) — 2026-05-22 Product Town Hall

## Observed
A single logo treatment does not work on both light and dark backgrounds across Community, Courses, and Portal.

## Why it matters
A single logo treatment produces visibly broken branding when member-facing surfaces switch between light and dark backgrounds.

## Expected
Support separate light and dark logo variants and use the appropriate asset automatically across member-facing surfaces.

## HighLevel response
No general cross-surface implementation verified.

## Official changelog verification — 2026-11-13

**Result:** Partial implementation — request remains open.

**Already available:** GoKollab web now supports independent light/dark palettes for Communities and Courses.

**Scope decision:** Automatic switching between distinct light/dark LOGO assets across Community/Courses/Portal is not documented.

**Official sources:** [Dark mode for Communities & Courses in GoKollab](https://ideas.gohighlevel.com/changelog/dark-mode-for-communities-courses-in-gokollab).

**Checked:** 2026-11-13. A feature announced only in Labs/private beta is not automatically generally available.
