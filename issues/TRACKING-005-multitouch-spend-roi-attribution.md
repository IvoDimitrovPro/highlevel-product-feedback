# TRACKING-005 — Deliver transparent multi-touch attribution with ad costs and true profitability

**Product:** Reporting
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** New
**Impact:** High
**Date reported:** 2026-10-30
**Source:** [HighLevel attribution rules](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/48001219997-understanding-attribution-traffic-sources); [HYROS U-shaped model](https://hyros.com/updates/power-features/new-attribution-model-u-shaped-attribution/); [RedTrack reporting and spend integrations](https://www.redtrack.io/platform/features/) — checked 2026-10-30

## Observed
HighLevel provides First/Latest contact attribution and funnel/external-site reports; the reviewed public docs do not demonstrate an independently queryable full-touch attribution model with channel-level cost reconciliation, historic model comparisons and verified profit across external platforms.

## Why it matters
Revenue without spend, refunds, attribution windows and intermediate touchpoints is not enough to allocate budget. A direct visit that follows a paid click must not erase earlier observed campaign evidence.

## Expected
Join available consented touchpoints with verifiable orders and ad spend from Meta, Google, TikTok and other documented connectors down to campaign/ad set/ad when authorized. Support first/last/first-paid/last-paid, linear, U-shaped, time decay and transparent optional data-driven models; selectable lookback windows, conversion-date versus click-date, cross-platform dedup, reportable unknown/unattributed buckets, spend-source freshness and FX provenance. Show gross/net revenue, CAC, CPA, ROAS, MER and margin when costs are known; never imply causal certainty or fabricate missing touchpoints. Offer API/export of underlying evidence and attribution-credit allocations, not just dashboards.

[Full product contract and acceptance tests](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/references/tracking-open-attribution-platform-2026-10-30.md).

## HighLevel response
No acceptance or committed plan for the complete requested comparison and profitability engine.
