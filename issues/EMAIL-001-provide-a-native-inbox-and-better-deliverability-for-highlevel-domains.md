# EMAIL-001 — Domains: Native inbox and deliverability

**Product:** Email / Domains
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** New
**Impact:** High
**Date reported:** 2026-05-01
**Source:** [Zoom feedback index](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/zoom-feedback-index-2026.md) — 2026-05-01 Product Town Hall

## Observed
Ivo repeatedly asked for an inbox for sold domains and reported spam placement/deliverability concerns.

## Why it matters
A managed-domain offer is incomplete if customers cannot reliably receive/reply to mail or diagnose delivery problems.

## Expected
Provide a native receive/reply inbox for domains managed or sold through HighLevel and give agencies clear deliverability/authentication diagnostics for those domains.

## HighLevel response
No resolution recorded for the domain inbox and deliverability gaps.

## Official changelog verification — 2026-11-13

**Result:** Partial implementation — request remains open.

**Already available:** LC Email beta includes dedicated-domain warm-up and SPF/DKIM/DMARC/MX and deliverability health reports.

**Scope decision:** A native receiving/replying inbox for HighLevel-managed/sold domains is not covered; beta may require approval.

**Official sources:** [Email Domain Warm-Up is here for LC Email (Beta)](https://ideas.gohighlevel.com/changelog/email-domain-warm-up-is-here-for-lc-email-beta).

**Checked:** 2026-11-13. A feature announced only in Labs/private beta is not automatically generally available.
