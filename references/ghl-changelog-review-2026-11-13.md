## Scope rules used

- A release headline is not sufficient to close a composite issue. Verify each requested behavior against the release text and current docs.
- **Labs/private beta** is partial availability, not general availability to every sub-account. Feature flags, account plans and rollout gates must be noted.
- Meeting Room's native **picture-in-picture** does not confirm *mobile* PiP. A calendar's unique meeting link does not by itself confirm attendance/recording/CRM outcome reconciliation. Native invoice layouts do not guarantee customer-supplied VAT fields.
- Paid channels can charge members and Community plans can bundle channels. This does **not** deliver COMM-005's binding to existing external Products/Offers, independent time-limited entitlements, multi-grant access precedence or attribution.
- Git Sync/AI Studio Cloud are real capabilities but do not confirm an externally authorized full AI Studio project-management or schema-migration API/MCP.
- The October 2026 Meta pixel release improves checkout events for configured surfaces but does not create the universal tracking/attribution system requested in TRACKING-001/002–007.
- Planned roadmap replies (including the November 2026 Meeting Room schedule) are not evidence of shipment.
